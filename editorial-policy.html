Editorial policy

Articles are designed to be original, useful, and clear about the limits of generalized guidance. The editorial process emphasizes business outcomes, human accountability, responsible data use, and claims that can be explained without promotional exaggeration. Material is reviewed for structure, accessibility, internal consistency, and source-independent reasoning before publication. Corrections may be made when an error or material ambiguity is identified. Generated visuals are original editorial illustrations or scenes created for the accompanying article and are not documentary evidence of an event.
